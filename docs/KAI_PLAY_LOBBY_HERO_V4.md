# KAI PLAY Lobby Hero V4

## Product route

- Primary archetype: Consumer App
- Task gravity: Operate
- Dominant object: a playable Dou Dizhu table
- Brand mode: explicit KAI, using the existing K mark and a new code-native wordmark

The Lobby opens directly on the table. The removed introductory headline and explanatory paragraph no longer compete with the game surface.

## Reference contract

The supplied screenshots were used only to study material thickness, felt response, card contact shadows and table-to-room separation.

Learn:

- five visible physical layers;
- a padded rail with thickness rather than a flat border;
- cards with fan, lift and contact shadow;
- a warm surrounding space that grounds the table.

Do not learn or copy:

- poker betting controls, chips, pots, cash values or casino branding;
- third-party table silhouettes, logos, chairs or decorative metalwork;
- neon gambling palettes.

## Material contract

The table is rendered from five ordered, non-interactive layers:

1. ambient grounding shadow;
2. leather table base and visible thickness;
3. graphite padded rail;
4. restrained coral inlay;
5. deep emerald felt playing surface.

The DOM owns all player names, cards, actions and state. No gameplay information is baked into the bitmap textures.

## Generated concept assets

Both material textures were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-08-26, then resized to 720 × 720 AVIF for local web delivery. They are original concept materials and do not prove the appearance of a manufactured physical table.

## Truth contract

- Lobby seats say that intelligent players join after the game starts.
- The primary CTA creates a real server-authoritative game.
- No online counts, active-room counts, friend presence or live countdown is fabricated.
- Daily puzzles remain visibly unavailable until their data and evaluation service exist.

## Responsive contract

- Desktop: 98 × 145 card fan in a 620px Hero.
- 390 class: 80 × 120 card fan with recomposed player positions.
- 320 class: 70 × 105 card fan, compact Wordmark and score, no document-level horizontal overflow.
- All primary touch controls remain at least 44 × 44 CSS pixels.
- Reduced motion removes decoration animation without hiding the table, cards or CTA.
