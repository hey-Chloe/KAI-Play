# KAI PLAY Card + Table V3

## Product route

- Primary archetype: Consumer App.
- Route modes: Lobby discovery, active-game operation, bright result review.
- Task gravity: `Operate` — sit down, understand turn state, choose a legal action, see the result.
- Dominant object: the authoritative Dou Dizhu table, not a marketing card.
- Brand mode: explicit KAI, limited to the existing K mark, disciplined state language, and an original KAI PLAY card back.

## Reference contract

Learn from the supplied references:

- clear seat → state → shared play area → own hand → controls hierarchy;
- premium felt depth, card material, readable face values and identifiable backs;
- a strong visual distinction between a bright lobby and an immersive play room.

Do not learn or copy:

- casino chips, pots, buy-ins, cash language, odds, neon gambling styling, avatars, brand marks or exact layouts;
- poker-specific five-seat geometry for a three-player Dou Dizhu game;
- unsupported presence, win probability or social activity.

## Art direction

Because KAI PLAY is a Chinese three-player card game with a one-click core loop, it uses a bright, lightweight lobby; a deep emerald, triangular play room; and a bright paper-like result view. Warm-white cards, graphite suits, restrained coral red, graphite rails and one original KAI PLAY geometric card back create tactile identity without casino gold or visual noise.

## System

- Card face: warm white paper, subtle grain, 1px highlight, restrained radius and ambient shadow.
- Black suits: graphite, not pure black. Red suits: deep coral, not vivid betting red.
- Court cards: modern KAI monogram panels rather than copied traditional illustrations.
- Card back: supplied local SVG asset with repeating K geometry and a central KAI PLAY medallion.
- Table: inline vector frame with deep felt, inner rail highlight, vignette and visible three-seat geometry.
- Typography: system UI stack for Chinese; system serif for card ranks only; tabular numerals for scores, counts and timers.
- Motion: deal, select, turn and score only; all state remains clear with reduced motion.

## Truth contract

- Lobby says intelligent players join after starting; it does not claim live presence.
- Game seats, roles, remaining-card counts, turn, hand, bottom cards, last action and settlement come from the server game view.
- Decorative lobby cards are marked as decorative; real read-only cards retain accessible labels.
- No chips, wagering, buy-in, withdrawal, cash value or fabricated online statistics.

## Responsive contract

- 1440px: full triangular table and readable card material.
- 390px: portrait table composition with all three seats, primary control and full hand visible.
- 320px: reflow without document-level horizontal scrolling; compressed overlap preserves all cards and 44px core actions.
